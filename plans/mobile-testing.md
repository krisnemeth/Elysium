# Mobile test round 1

What to try on real devices after round 1 of `plans/mobile.md`. Each item says what should happen; note anything that doesn't, with the device and the page. Signed in with your own account on the preview.

**Devices:** your iPhone in Safari (the most important), an Android phone in Chrome if you have one, an iPad or tablet, and a laptop for the desktop checks. On the iPhone, try with Safari's toolbar both at the bottom and at the top, if you use both.

**How to report:** a line per problem, e.g. "iPhone, Classic, Skills tab: the specialty field is hidden by the keyboard". A screenshot helps for anything visual. Mark each as *broken* (doesn't work), *wrong* (works, looks or feels off) or *idea*.

## 1. Moving around (phone)

- [ ] There's no bottom tab bar in a game. The menu button at the top opens the drawer from the right with every link; it closes by tapping outside or on a link.
- [ ] The landing's navbar is the menu button below laptop width, including a half-screen browser window on your Mac.
- [ ] Taps feel answered (a tick on iPhone 18+ / Android), and buttons show their colour while pressed.

## 2. Tapping small things (phone)

Nothing should have grown; small things should just be easier to hit.

- [ ] Rating dots (Classic, Attributes and Skills): tap a dot, then tap slightly above or below it, and in the gap between two dots. Each lands on a dot of that row, never the row above or below.
- [ ] Drag a finger sideways along a row of dots: the rating follows your finger. Dragging up or down scrolls the page instead and leaves the rating alone.
- [ ] Skill specialty fields are easy to tap into.
- [ ] Guided creation's thin step bar: tap a finished step to jump back.
- [ ] Small links ("All Kindred", "Full sheet", "Create an account", "Start over") are easy to hit.
- [ ] Landing: the character ticks on the rail under the logos are a little further apart than on desktop and easy to tap.

## 3. Character sheets (phone)

- [ ] Open a character: a portrait banner, the name, summary and buttons (Edit sheet, Play, Delete; no PDF), then tabs starting at Profile.
- [ ] Only one section shows at a time. Tapping a tab switches; swiping left or right on the section moves to the next or previous one (with a tick).
- [ ] Swiping on a row of dots changes the rating, not the section.
- [ ] The tabs stay under the top bar while you scroll, and the active tab scrolls into view.
- [ ] Edit sheet (Classic): the same tabs. Change a few things in different sections, leave, come back: everything saved.
- [ ] Tap a trait's dotted name (e.g. Strength): its explanation rises at the bottom of the screen, fully inside it. Tapping elsewhere closes it.
- [ ] On the iPad, the sheet is the long page as before, with the tabs jumping down it.

## 4. Character creation (phone)

- [ ] Guided: Back, Start over and Continue stay at the bottom of the screen on long steps, clear of the home indicator.
- [ ] Type into fields near the bottom of a step: the keyboard doesn't cover the field or leave the buttons floating in the middle of the screen.
- [ ] Merits and flaws: is the list workable on the phone? (A searchable list is planned for round 2 if not.)
- [ ] Finish a character and land on its sheet.

## 5. Play mode (phone)

- [ ] Two tabs, Condition and Roll. Condition: Health and Willpower boxes, Hunger/Rage/Desperation, Humanity or the rest; all easy to mark.
- [ ] Mark damage, switch to Roll: the pool reflects being impaired.
- [ ] Roll: the tray first, then the steppers, result and buttons. Rolls feel smooth (note if the dice stutter, and which phone).
- [ ] Leave and come back: the damage you marked is still there.

## 6. Dice page

- [ ] Phone: the tray on top, then pool / Hunger / difficulty in one row, the result, the check, Reroll, reset and Roll; Recent rolls below. Note whether Roll is on screen without scrolling.
- [ ] Recent rolls only shows a roll once the dice have landed.
- [ ] Laptop: the whole page fits the window for all three games, with no scrolling. Recent rolls scrolls inside its card after many rolls.
- [ ] Willpower reroll: after a roll, tap up to three dice in the tray, then Reroll.

## 7. Loresheets (phone)

- [ ] The list, filtered by game; open one.
- [ ] Long fields grow as you type rather than scrolling inside a small box (iPhone needs iOS 26 for this; on older versions they stay fixed-height, which is fine).
- [ ] The save status sits under the top bar; Delete works and asks first.

## 8. Character images (phone)

- [ ] On a sheet, the portrait's Upload and Choose from library buttons are visible without hovering.
- [ ] Upload offers the camera and your photos. Framing: drag to move, pinch to zoom, then Save; the new portrait shows.
- [ ] Choose from library: a grid you can scroll and pick from.

## 9. The rest (phone)

- [ ] Overview: greeting, the three game cards (tap a card to open the game; tap its count to unfold its characters), drafts and loresheets.
- [ ] Coterie/Pack/Cell: shows the List view on the phone (Grid on the laptop) until you choose; your choice sticks.
- [ ] Werewolf home: the pack first, two to a row, then the moons and caerns.
- [ ] Settings: the haptics switch works and is remembered on this phone.
- [ ] Friends: send and accept a request.
- [ ] Login and signup: the keyboard doesn't hide the button; password autofill works.
- [ ] Open a chronicle (or a link to one): the phone shows "The table needs a larger screen", who's there, and Copy the link. The iPad still opens the table. An invitation can still be accepted on the phone.

## 10. Landing (phone and laptop)

- [ ] Phone: the clips (Creation, Themes, Dice) play by themselves when mostly on screen, one at a time; the corner button pauses one, and it stays paused until you scroll past.
- [ ] Laptop: the Dice section is two columns, the three games' dice on the left (each opens its roller), the clip on the right: the Vampire still until you hover, then all three rollers in turn.
- [ ] Swipe the cover on the phone to turn the characters.
